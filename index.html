FAQ

We answer your questions.

Cannot find yours? Message us and we reply within one business day.

How much does a website cost?

Our websites start from $100 (about Rp 1.600.000). We don't use fixed packages or price tiers. The final price depends on your scope, and we shape it around your needs.

Is there a monthly fee?

Your estimate lists everything that is included. If you need ongoing support, hosting or updates, we can add them based on what you need.

Which website types do you build?

Company profile, catalog, news and blog, and landing page websites. If you need something different, tell us and we will find the right fit.

Do I own the design and content?

Ownership terms are written in your estimate before we start, so everything is clear from day one.

How long until my website is ready?

It depends on the scope and how quickly your content is ready. You get a clear timeline with your estimate.

Are the domain and hosting included?

We can include them or work with the ones you already have. We confirm this in your estimate.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile first and tested on desktop, tablet and phone.

How do I get started?

Send us a message with your goals. We reply within one business day with questions and an estimate.
